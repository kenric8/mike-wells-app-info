Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

Mike Wells Transcription is a personal-use application for converting authorized audio recordings of Christian teachings into text transcripts.

Information Accessed

The application accesses audio files from an authorized Dropbox account and uses Google Drive authorization to check for and store completed transcripts.

How Information Is Used

Audio recordings are processed using an open-source speech recognition model running in a private automation environment. Completed transcripts are saved to the authorized Google Drive account.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use accessed data for advertising. Processing infrastructure and cloud storage providers may handle data as necessary to operate the transcription workflow.

Data Storage and Security

Original audio recordings remain in Dropbox. Transcripts are stored in Google Drive. Temporary processing files are created during transcription. Access credentials are stored as private automation secrets.

Access and Revocation

The account owner may revoke Google access through Google Account security settings and Dropbox access through Dropbox connected-app settings.

Contact

Questions about this application or privacy policy can be directed to the application support email listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
